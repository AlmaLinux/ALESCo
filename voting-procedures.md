# Roll Call Voting

All voting is done via a Roll-Call Vote, so each vote is recorded individually and published publicly. For RFC and Chair votes, we use a Simple Majority Vote. The majority is defined as more than 50% of votes cast, excluding abstentions.

## Procedures

Below are the procedures ALESCo will follow for all official votes.

1. The Chair will state the voting issue before the vote.
2. The Chair will list all voting members present and eligible to vote for the issue and the number of votes required to define the majority.
3. The Chair will address each voting member and ask for a vote of "Yes", "No", or "Abstain."
    * Members who abstain will not be included in the tally when determining if a majority has been reached. **The majority is defined as more than 50% of votes cast, excluding abstentions.**
4. The Chair will count the votes.
5. The Chair will announce the results.
    * If a decision receives more than 50% of the votes cast (excluding abstentions), the decision passes.
    * If the decision receives 50% or fewer of the votes cast (excluding abstentions), the decision is indecisive and can be voted on in the future.

# Example Vote

Here's a hypothetical example of the Roll Call Voting procedure.

**Voting Issue:** Should ALESCo adopt new voting procedures?

### Procedures Followed:

1. **Announcement by the Chair:**

> "The voting issue before us is whether to adopt new voting procedures as outlined in RFC #XYZ."

2. **Listing of Voting Members:**
 - The Chair lists the following voting members present in alphabetical order:
   - Member A
   - Member B
   - Member C
   - Member D
   - Member E
   - Total voting members present: 5
   - Majority required to pass: 3 votes (which is greater than 50% of 5).

3. **Roll Call Voting:**
 - The Chair addresses each voting member:

 > Chair: "Member A, what is your vote?"
>
 > Member A: "Yes"
 >
 > Chair: "Member B, what is your vote?"

 > Member B: "No."
     
 > Chair: "Member C, what is your vote?"
     
 > Member C: "Yes."
     
 > Chair: "Member D, what is your vote?"
     
 > Member D: "Abstain."
     
 > Chair: "Member E, what is your vote?"
     
 > Member E: "Yes."

4. **Counting the Votes:**

 - Votes recorded: 
   - Yes: 3
   - No: 1
   - Abstain: 1

5. **Announcement of Results:**

> The results are as follows: Yes – 3, No – 1, Abstain – 1. With 3 Yes votes out of 4 votes cast, the motion has received a majority. Therefore, the motion passes.

# Example Vote: Election of a New Chair

Voting Issue: Election of a new Chair for ALESCo.

## Procedures Followed:

1. **Announcement by the Chair:**

> "The voting issue before us is the election of a new Chair for ALESCo. This election will follow the procedures outlined in the ALESCo Voting Policy, using a roll-call vote and requiring a simple majority to elect."

2. **Verification of Nominations:**

> "Before proceeding to the vote, I will confirm whether each voting member has submitted or would like to declare a nomination for Chair."
>
> Chair: "Member A, do you have a nomination to declare?"
>
> Member A: "Yes – I nominate Member E."
>
> Chair: "Member E, you've been nominated. Do you accept the nomination?"
>
> Member E: "Yes, I accept the nomination" 
>
> Chair: "Member B, do you have a nomination to declare?"
>
> Member B: "No"
>
> Chair: "Member C, do you have a nomination to declare?"
>
> Member C: "Yes – I nominate myself."
>
> Chair: "Member D, do you have a nomination to declare?"
>
> Member D: "No"
>
> Chair: "Member E, do you have a nomination to declare?"
>
> Member E: "No"
>
> Chair: "Thank you. Nominations have been confirmed. The candidates for Chair are Member C and Member E. We will now proceed to the vote."

3. **Listing of Voting Members:**

> "The following voting members are present and eligible to vote, listed in alphabetical order:"
>
> * Member A
> * Member B
> * Member C
> * Member D
> * Member E

> "There are 5 voting members present. A simple majority requires at least 3 votes for a candidate to be elected."

4. **Roll Call Voting:**

> Chair: "Please vote for your preferred candidate by stating either 'Member C', 'Member E', or 'Abstain'."

> Chair: "Member A, who is your vote for Chair?"
>
> Member A: "Member E"
> 
> Chair: "Member B, who is your vote for Chair?"
>
> Member B: "Member C"
>
> Chair: "Member C, who is your vote for Chair?"
>
> Member C: "Member C"
> 
> Chair: "Member D, who is your vote for Chair?"
>
> Member D: "Abstain"
>
> Chair: "Member E, who is your vote for Chair?"
>
> Member E: "Member E"

5. **Counting the Votes:**

 - Votes recorded
   - Votes for Member C: 2
   - Votes for Member E: 2
   - Abstain: 1
   - Total votes cast (excluding abstention): 4

6. **Announcement of Results:**

> "The results are as follows: Member C – 2 votes, Member E – 2 votes, Abstain – 1. With no candidate receiving a majority of the 4 votes cast, no Chair has been elected. The decision is inconclusive and will be revisited in a future vote."
